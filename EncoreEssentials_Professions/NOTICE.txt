Ideas and code from other addons
  What's Training? (Fusionpit, https://github.com/fusionpit/WhatsTraining, MIT): the
  way recipes are chosen (only trainer recipes and vendor recipes bought for gold, no
  drops, quests, reputation or Merchant's Favor) and the rank data in Data/Recipes.lua
  (Journeyman, Expert and Artisan: skill, level, fee, and the book or quest that teaches
  each), plus the learn skill of a few recipes ForeverDB has none for.
  SkillUp Forever (cjber, https://github.com/cjber/skillup-forever, GPL-3.0-or-later):
  the route planner idea (planned once, planned again on request), how the Working
  Overtime Legacy talent is read (Core.lua) and its +4% per point estimate, how the
  Auctionator shopping list is written (Shopping.lua), and the vendor reagent prices in
  Data/Recipes.lua (ns.VendorPrices: the client's buy prices; the item list comes from
  LibPeriodicTable-3.1, LGPL-2.1). Its trainer, quest and drop data come from CMaNGOS and
  are not used.
  GatherLite (Wuild, https://github.com/Wuild/GatherLite, MIT): herb and ore skill
  levels, node places per zone and fish caught per zone in Data/World.lua. Show nodes
  calls GatherLite's own SetNodeTracking when GatherLite is installed.

Data
  Recipes, skill colours, reagents, trainer fees and recipe vendors with their places
  come from Encore-DB: Data from ForeverDB.net (https://foreverdb.net), GPL-3.0 with
  ForeverDB.net's attribution term (see EncoreEssentials_DB/NOTICE.txt), checked against
  Wowhead Forever (https://www.wowhead.com/forever).
  Profession trainers and their places, the NPCs and quests behind the rank books and
  quests, zone maps, beasts and mob levels per zone: QuestieDB
  (https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB, GPL-3.0), its WoW Forever data.
  tools/professions/build.py builds both data files; nothing is copied by hand.
