1. ForeverDB.net
   Data from ForeverDB.net (https://foreverdb.net)

   Data/Loot.lua and Data/Recipes.lua are built from the ForeverDB addon's
   data files (Loot.lua, Recipes.lua, Items.lua, NPCs.lua, Dungeons.lua,
   SearchNames.lua),
   Copyright (C) 2026 ForeverDB.net, licensed under GPL-3.0 with an
   attribution term under section 7(b): keep the notices in the files,
   including the first line of each file
     -- Data: ForeverDB.net (https://foreverdb.net), build <client build>. ...
   and credit "Data from ForeverDB.net (https://foreverdb.net)" where the work
   lists its credits and sources. Anyone reusing these files must do the
   same.

   Encore-DB keeps only what ForeverDB labels as seen in WoW Forever (drops,
   drop rates per looted kill, vendors, quests) and what it reads from the WoW
   Forever client. ForeverDB's Classic data (from the CMaNGOS classic-db),
   its unconfirmed client clues and the recipes it marks as speculation are
   left out.

2. Quests and boss levels: the QuestieDB addon
   Encore-DB reads its dungeon quests (titles, levels, objectives, quest
   givers, chains, reward items, reputation and XP) and its boss levels in
   the game from the QuestieDB addon (https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB)
   by the Questie team, GPL-3.0, through its public API (QuestieLive.lua).
   None of QuestieDB's data is bundled with Encore-DB: install QuestieDB
   (a required dependency on CurseForge) and you get its updates directly.
   QuestieDB's WoW Forever database is Questie's Classic data (originally
   built from CMaNGOS) converted to Forever's maps, plus Forever
   corrections, QuestieTrace recordings and hand-written fixes. Thank you
   to the Questie team.

3. Fill-in sources
   foreverchanges.pro (https://foreverchanges.pro) adds bosses, items and
   instances that ForeverDB has no Forever data for yet, and recipe sources
   from its item pages.
   Wowhead Forever (https://www.wowhead.com/forever) gives item details
   (names, qualities, icons, slots and levels), fills in boss drops and
   drop chances ForeverDB has not seen, reputation rewards, world drops and
   quest details QuestieDB leaves blank, and every recipe is checked against
   it. Thank you to Wowhead.
   AtlasLoot Continued (by Okrah, Lag and many others; its WoW Forever port)
   is read during the build as the list of drops to show for each boss it
   covers (tools/loot/atlasloot-loot.mjs). Each drop is looked up on Wowhead
   Forever, and only what Wowhead confirms is listed, as Wowhead data. None of AtlasLoot's files or data are included:
   AtlasLoot is GPL v2 only, which can't be combined with GPL-3.0.
   Data/Extras.lua (Warsong Gulch and Alterac Valley rewards, honor rewards,
   more reputation rewards, the Collections tab, item sets and rogue poisons)
   is Wowhead Forever data: names, levels, prices, vendors, sources, the
   reputation each item needs, item sets and the poison recipes
   (tools/loot/build-extras.mjs). AtlasLoot Continued's PvP, Collections and
   Factions lists are read only to know which items to look up
   (tools/loot/atlasloot-extras.mjs); an item is listed only when Wowhead
   Forever shows a source for it in WoW Forever. None of AtlasLoot's data is
   included.
   Boss levels in Data/Loot.lua come from Wowhead Forever's NPC pages; in
   the game the QuestieDB addon's Forever NPC levels replace them where it
   knows the boss (section 2). Only the levels and
   boss rank are used.

4. Boss portraits: Dungeon Journal
   The boss portrait pictures in Media/Portraits come from Dungeon Journal
   (ForeverDungeonJournal) by Exehn, which photographed the game's own
   portraits in WoW Forever. They are renamed to each boss's NPC ID; the
   pictures themselves are unchanged. Data/Portraits.lua lists them and was
   made with tools/import-dj-portraits.mjs. Thank you, Exehn.
