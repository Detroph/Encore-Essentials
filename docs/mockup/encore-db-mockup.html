Layout

  • Main categories are tabs across the top. The left tree shows only the open tab.
  • Dungeons are grouped into level brackets. 20- and 40-player raids share one list with a size tag.
  • The middle column adapts: bosses for instances, skill brackets for professions, standings for factions.

Encore look

  • Encore crest without text as the window portrait and minimap button.
  • Clean dark finish inside the copper frame, with teal gems on the corners.
  • Red Encore tabs, red buttons, gold game text with black outline, quest-log +/− headers.
  • In-game, frames, icons and the Friz Quadrata font come from the game itself. This page imitates them.

Recipes (real data)

  • Professions › Alchemy: click any recipe to open it.
  • Hover the recipe, crafted item and reagent icons for their tooltips.
  • Recipes, reagents and skill levels come from the WoW Forever client.
  • Trainers, vendors and drops are not collected yet. They will come from Forever itself, with zone, map coordinates and a faction filter.

Forever marking

  • No separate Forever category. New items stay where they drop.
  • Teal frame, teal background and a teal gem on the item, using the crest's teal.
  • Teal counters on tabs, tree entries and bosses. "Forever only" filters every view.
